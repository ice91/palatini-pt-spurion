## Dynamics and excluded irreps

The Hessian here is differentiation with respect to connection/A components at a point. Its nullity does not count propagating metric/scalar degrees of freedom or first/second-class constraints. No spacetime kinetic Hessian, ADM second variation, physical Poisson/Dirac algebra, characteristic cone, ghost analysis, or energy estimate was derived. These are outside the source's stated theorem; they must not be inferred from the auxiliary rank.

The reduced scalar coefficient is in a nonminimally coupled scalar-metric action. Its algebraic value alone is not a stability criterion. If phi is fixed background data there is no scalar Cauchy theorem at all. If phi is varied, the reduced bulk equations are those of the displayed reduced action with consistent boundary treatment; this still does not decide their well-posedness or propagating content.

Deleted torsion/nonmetricity irreps are neither projectively gauged away nor dynamically eliminated by this calculation. Restoring them would change the configuration domain and require another analysis. This audit does not perform or recommend that extension as a prerequisite for the manuscript's already limited theorem.
