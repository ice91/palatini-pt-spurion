                                    6. The Final Pillar: Coefficient Locking and Exact Luminality (C3)

                                    unique linear combination of bulk-equivalent routes. Under the admissible-domain posture, this

                                    structural sufficiency—no parameter tuning—within the stated assumptions; beyond-quadratic or
