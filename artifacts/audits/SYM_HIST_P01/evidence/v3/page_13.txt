                   5.3. Implications of the Equivalence
                        The three-route equivalence (C2) serves as a powerful consistency check, demonstrating the
                   robustness and internal coherence of the framework. More importantly, it furnishes us with a set
                   of distinct but bulk-equivalent building blocks (LROD,LCM). While identical in the bulk, their full
                   expressions (including boundary terms) couple differently to metric perturbations. This distinction is
                   thecrucialingredientthatenablesthecoefficient-lockingmechanismforachievingexactluminality,as
                   we will demonstrate in the next section.
