                  4.2. Implications and Diagnostics
                        The uniqueness theorem C1 is a cornerstone of our framework. It demonstrates that the axial and
                  tensor components of torsion are not merely suppressed, but are algebraically eliminated from the
                  observablesectorbythedynamics. Thisisapowerfulmechanismforavoidingtheextrapropagating
                  degrees of freedom that often plague theories with torsion.

                  Corollary (Basis Reduction)
                        As a direct consequence, the "pre-lock" operator basis {I1,I2,I3} from Sec.3.2dynamically
                  collapsesintoa"post-lock"basiswheretheoperatorsarelinearlydependent. Therelation(11)      shows
                  thatonlyoneindependentoperatorstructure,describablebyeither IT orΣϵ,remainsatthisorder. This
                  profound simplification is the key to the bulk equivalences we will explore in the next section.
