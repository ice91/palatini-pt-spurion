                    11.2. Operational Disambiguation Of A1–A5
                    A1 (domain/measure).
                           “R X = R PT[X]” means:  work on oriented Lorentzian patches with the standard measure
                    d4x√−g and PT-invariant boundaries (compact or AF/FRW fall-offs).  The pullback by PT pre-

                    (Theorem1) and fixes parity assignments in the Holst/Nieh–Yan sector.

                    A4 (boundary posture).
                           We adopt either compact PT-invariant boundaries with vanishing flux or standard AF/FRW

                    This makes statements like K−G = ∂µ(a−3Jµ∆a3) operationally slicing/gauge independent at quadratic
