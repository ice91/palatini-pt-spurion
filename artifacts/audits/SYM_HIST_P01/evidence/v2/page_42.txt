                   Appendix E.3. Nieh–Yan as a Boundary Convention (A5)
                         TheNieh–Yan4-formNY≡ d(eA∧TA) isanexactform. Inourposture(A5),anyexplicituseof
                   NY enters only as a boundary convention after applying the scalar projectorΠPT: it does not modify
                   Euler–Lagrange equations in the bulk and is indistinguishable from a choice of improvement current.
                   This applies equally to (i) the three-chain equivalence (where the PT-even CS/Nieh–Yan shadow
                   differs from the DBI/CM routes by an improvement current) and (ii) the Dirac sector (where vector
                   rephasing reshufflesboundary terms that can beabsorbed into the chosenNieh–Yan convention). No
                   physical statement in Sections5–8depends on a specific Nieh–Yan choice.
