## Recovered-derivation threshold

A purported recovery is counted as a full physical derivation only if it supplies the same original action and conventions, states assumptions, evaluates the necessary variations, reduces the physical constraints and produces the claimed coefficients/observable. None of the historical sources meets this threshold for C1(unaugmented), C2(three routes), C3(physical K,G/locking) or NLO(unique operator).

Narrow, valid recovered steps have explicit present/missing boundaries:

1. **Multiplier variation** — exact source/equations and independent multiplier assumption in02/H03. This step is complete for that added constraint; it is not a full action-equivalence proof.
2. **Raw determinant Taylor term** — old AppendixC supplies a genuine algebraic calculation under its assumed trace identities. It is valid on the matching spacelike/canonical domain; old IT conversion and literal timelike definition are inconsistent. See04/H12–13.
3. **Classical infinite-mass stationary limit** — follows from the written trace equation for fixed coefficients and nonsingular limit; the generic finite-mass equality does not. See03/H08–09.
4. **Constraint inventory / tensor-level NLO ansatz** — additional text/equations are recovered, but missing brackets/source variations are not replaced by those assertions. See04/H17–18.

These limited recoveries materially sharpen the historical diagnosis. They are not new proofs manufactured from old claims and do not meet final decisionA.
