## 2. Parent: derivative algebra of one massless scalar

Set c=ℏ=k_B=1, η=diag(+1,−1), x∈R. The classical seed is

\[
 S[\phi]=\frac12\int_{\mathbb R^{1,1}}dt\,dx\,[(\partial_t\phi)^2-(\partial_x\phi)^2],
 \qquad (\partial_t^2-\partial_x^2)\phi=0.                 \tag{1}
\]

The actual quantum observables are smeared currents j_μ=∂_μφ and bounded Weyl operators generated by these currents. Equivalently one can use φ smeared on the zero-integral test-function domain, modulo its equation and constant mode. **No vacuum for an unrestricted unsmeared massless φ or independent zero mode is asserted.** Smooth compactly supported current smearings define the local algebra A(O); the global vacuum representation of the current theory is the usual irreducible Fock representation.

The convenient distributional mode notation is

\[
 \phi(t,x)=\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{dk}{\sqrt{4\pi|k|}}
  (a_ke^{-i|k|t+ikx}+a_k^\dagger e^{i|k|t-ikx}),\quad
 [a_k,a_{k'}^\dagger]=\delta(k-k'),\quad a_k|0_M\rangle=0.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]

Only admissible smearing/derivatives of (2) are observables. The normal ordered generator

\[
 H_M=\int dk\,|k|a_k^\dagger a_k\geq0,\quad
 U_M(t)=e^{-iH_Mt},\quad U_M(t)|0_M\rangle=|0_M\rangle             \tag{3}
\]

fixes globally reversible evolution and a pure global state ω_M(A)=〈0_M|A|0_M〉. Wick's rule fixes higher correlators. No statistical mixture, temperature, spacetime-dependent constitutive function or phenomenological kernel is supplied.

On the formal scalar distribution, with E=G_ret−G_adv,

\[
 [\phi(t,x),\phi(0,0)]=-iE(t,x),\quad
 E=\tfrac12\operatorname{sgn}(t)\Theta(t^2-x^2).               \tag{4}
\]

For currents differentiate (4) and smear; equal-time canonical normalization is [φ(t,x),∂_tφ(t,y)]=iδ(x−y) on the admissible domain. In particular spacelike local algebras commute. This fixes the sign conventions used for response in report 03.
