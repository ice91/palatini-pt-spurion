# I06 — operational cones do not require full simplicity

**Decision:** a controlled counterexample exists in one real Lorentzian non-chiral family. Relaxing one invariant multiplier direction leaves nineteen algebraic constraints and produces a metric plus a healthy massive scalar on a regular positive branch. The directly B-defined electromagnetic cone is independent of that scalar. Thus healthy local propagation and this operational cone do not force hard Plebanski simplicity. Exactly two total modes at generic points is a stronger criterion and does select the hard limit within this particular family; merely having a two-tensor low-energy sector does not.

## 3. Typed maps: a real construction and a failure of dynamical projection

Let X_+ be the local B-field manifold satisfying C19 on the chosen nondegenerate, positive-orientation gravitational chart, with |β|<1. Internal-frame gauge may be quotiented, with spacetime diffeomorphism covariance kept explicit.

All B=(star+β)Σ(e) with fixed e have the same π(B). It is not an endomorphism of X_+, so π²=π is not even a well-typed statement. It loses data relevant to gravity.
2. **Algebraic hard retraction:** R₀:X_+→X_hard⊂X_+ sends B↦starΣ(e(B)), holding the reconstructed coframe fixed. It is nonlinear, real and covariant on this specified sheet, and R₀²=R₀. It preserves J_B. Its choice to set β=0 is additional selection, not compelled by probe admissibility.

In the exact initial data (30), removing β and its energy while retaining γ_ij,K_ij leaves Hamiltonian residual 6H²≠0. Thus the retraction does not preserve the physical constraint surface of initial data or commute with evolution. A further geometry-changing constraint solve would require choices; none is invented.

## 4. Chiral versus non-chiral controls

Alexandrov–Krasnov, [0809.4763](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.4763), §3, finds eight generic non-chiral modes when the multiplier-potential Hessian has the requisite full rank; its concluding discussion explicitly makes the count depend on that Hessian. Our one-trace square has rank one. Applying the generic count, or assigning its extra spin-two/scalar ghosts to our single β without calculation, would be incorrect. The exact reduction and nonvanishing scalar Legendre Hessian supply our three-mode count.

Krasnov, [0811.3147](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.3147), Eqs.(2)–(8) and §§II–IV, constructs modified **chiral** theories retaining two modes. Its fields are an SO(3) triple, complexified in Lorentz signature with additional reality conditions. Those are not our real six-component SO⁺(1,3) bivectors. The paper is a warning against universal cross-domain uniqueness claims, **not** a proved real-Lorentzian healthy counterexample inside this declared one-parameter family. We do not claim every chiral potential has a controlled positive real Lorentz completion.

The reduced kinetic structure is an established varying-Holst benchmark. This sprint's executed results are the rank-one deformation calculation, coefficient/potential extraction, typed B–Maxwell map, generic-rank reduction and operational counterexamples. They are not promoted to new universal laws or to a new microscopic mechanism.
